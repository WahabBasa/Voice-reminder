## Product decisions (final)
- The X is Apple's AlarmKit banner when the phone is unlocked: timer icon = Later, X = Stop. Stop = Done for THAT occurrence (already the native mapping); the card must reflect it promptly, not on the next foreground.
- Later = ring 5 minutes FROM THE TAP, unlimited times. The 3-comeback cap applies only to IGNORED rings (auto-nag at +5/+10/+15 after the ring), after which the card shows "Missed".
- Done finishes that occurrence only; other occurrences the same day stay owed.
- While a ring is unresolved the card does not flip to Overdue. Resolution = Stop/Done, Later, or the nag chain exhausted (→ Missed).

## Plan
**Lifecycle contract** (new `lib/ringLifecycle.ts`, AsyncStorage-persisted, pure functions + a small store): records keyed `${reminderId}:${occurrenceAt}` = `{ reminderId, occurrenceAt, state: "ringing" | "snoozed" | "done" | "missed", snoozeUntil?, chainStep?: number, resolvedAt?, source: "alarmkit" | "fallback" }`. `markRinging(occ)` when the scheduled time passes for an armed occurrence (JS-side, from schedule + now; or from native "alerting" state), `markSnoozed(occ, until)`, `markDone(occ)`, `markMissed(occ)`; `resolveCardState(reminder, now, records)` → the card resolver input. Records older than 2 days pruned.
**Card** (`lib/remindersMembership.ts`, `lib/time.ts`, `components/ReminderListItem.tsx`): consult lifecycle before the overdue rule: ringing → "Ringing now" (no red, pinned near top); snoozed → "Rings again 3:57 pm"; done → treated as completed occurrence (one-off removed / repeater advances); missed → "Missed · 3:52 pm" red (today's overdue look) — one-offs only, as today. Unknown (no record, time passed) → keep today's behavior but only after `occurrenceAt + 16 min` (nag chain exhausted) so an unobserved ring isn't red instantly.
**Propagation**: (a) JS drains the native event log on the 30 s tick AND on foreground AND when native emits `VRAlarmEvent` (new RN event from the intents/app process via NotificationCenter → bridge); drain becomes apply-then-ack (idempotent) instead of delete-first. (b) On foreground and on tick, JS asks native `getAlarmStates()` → snapshot of `AlarmManager.shared.alarms` (id, state: scheduled/countdown/alerting/paused, fire date) to mark "ringing" from truth, and subscribes to `alarmUpdates` while the app runs (native forwards changes as `VRAlarmEvent`).
**Later semantics** (native intents, `plugins/ios-src/VRAlarmIntents.swift`): on Snooze intent: keep the guard-first order; cancel the remaining pre-armed nag siblings for this occurrence; arm ONE comeback at `now + 5 min` (with its own nag siblings at +5/+10 relative to it, so an ignored comeback still nags); record `snoozed` with `snoozeUntil = now + 5 min` (the real armed time). On Stop: record `done` for the occurrence, cancel its siblings (as today). Both post the NotificationCenter event after the durable write.
**Done unification**: card Done while ringing → stop the native alarm for that occurrence + cancel its chain, record done, one-off removed / repeater advances; the fallback overlay/notification Done routes through the same function. Notification *dismissal* on the notifee fallback route stays as-is (it is not a deliberate action).
**Not changing**: nag cadence for ignored rings, guard-before-stop and background intents (lock-screen Later without Face ID), sound pipeline, grid-first scheduling.
