2. What VoiceReminder is

VoiceReminder is a reminder app you talk to. You speak a reminder, the app turns your speech into text, works out what you meant and when it should fire, then generates a spoken version to play back at you when the time comes. You can also type reminders instead.

You don't need an account. The app needs microphone access to record and notification permission to alert you; without those, parts of it won't work.

Always check reminders that matter. After you create one, confirm the time, date, and wording on screen. You are responsible for verifying that a reminder says what you meant and is set for when you need it.
