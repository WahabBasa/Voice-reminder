**3. Purpose and target audience**

Remi is a voice-first reminder app. The user speaks a reminder in natural language
("remind me to take my medication at 8 pm every day"); the app transcribes the
recording, extracts the title and schedule, and creates the reminder automatically.
When the reminder is due, it rings and speaks the reminder aloud in a natural voice
instead of showing a silent banner.

The problem it solves: typing out reminders is high-friction, so people skip
capturing them, and silent notifications are easy to miss or dismiss without
registering. Remi makes capture take a few seconds of speech and makes delivery
impossible to miss. Target audience: busy adults who rely on reminders throughout the
day — including people who struggle with silent notifications (e.g. attention
difficulties) and users who prefer speaking to typing.

Business model: free to download and use, with an optional auto-renewable
subscription (Remi Pro, monthly or annual) that unlocks premium scheduling features.
No ads, no other monetization.

**4. Setup and access instructions**

No account, login, or sample files are required. The app is fully usable immediately:

1. Launch the app. Accept the one-time AI-processing consent sheet.
2. Allow microphone access and notification/alarm access when prompted.
3. Tap the microphone button and speak a reminder, e.g. "remind me to drink water in
   five minutes." The reminder appears on the home screen within a few seconds.
4. At the scheduled time the reminder rings and speaks aloud. "Later" snoozes it;
   "Done" completes it.
5. Subscription (Remi Pro): open Settings → the upgrade row opens the paywall, which
   shows the monthly and annual plans with title, length, and price (the annual plan
   includes a 7-day free trial), plus links to the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy.
   The paywall is also shown when a free user selects premium scheduling options.
   Restore Purchases is available in Settings, outside the paywall.

**5. External services used**

- Convex — backend database and server functions (reminder storage and processing)
- OpenAI — speech-to-text (Whisper) to transcribe the user's recording, and a GPT
  model to parse the transcription into a structured reminder (title, time, schedule)
- Speechify — text-to-speech that generates the spoken reminder audio (primary voice
  provider)
- ElevenLabs — text-to-speech fallback voice provider
- RevenueCat — subscription management on top of Apple In-App Purchase / StoreKit
- Sentry — crash and error reporting

All voice recordings are processed solely to deliver the reminder feature, as
disclosed in the app's privacy nutrition labels, the microphone purpose string, and
the first-run consent sheet. The app does no tracking (NSPrivacyTracking is false)
and contains no third-party ads.

**6. Regional differences**

None. The app's features and content are identical in all regions. The interface is
English at launch.

**7. Regulated industry / protected third-party material**

Not applicable. Remi is a general-purpose reminder utility; it does not operate in a
regulated industry and contains no protected third-party material. All reminder audio
is generated by the licensed text-to-speech services listed above under their
commercial API terms.
