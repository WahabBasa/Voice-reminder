# Competitor reviews — Talking Alarm Clock (Sentry Apps) + Alarm Clock Beyond

**Source**: Google Play reviews shared by the user 2026-08-19, supplementing the corpus that
locked the copy register in [aso-listing-playbook.md](aso-listing-playbook.md) on 08-16.
**Use**: the users' own words for captions, descriptions, and feature priorities. Quote them,
don't paraphrase them.

## Themes, ranked by how hard reviews reward/punish them

### 1. "The alarm tells me what it's for" — the core value, in their words
- "the alarm actually will tell me what the reminder is for" (Debi)
- "it has audio of alarm purpose" (Robert)
- "I can have the alarm say 'feed the dog', 'leave for doctor appointment', 'take your meds', 'it's Susan's birthday today'" (D. Hanc.)
- "hearing the voice tell me the date and time as well as my current task" (Pamela)
- "reminds me why it's waking me up" (CF Bennett)
- "When I can't get to my phone I hear it speaking the note I wrote" (Maria)

### 2. Repeats-until-done is a loved, searched-for behavior
- "tell me the time every five minutes until I dismissed it… then auto snoozes and tells me again" (top review, 348 helpful)
- "remind me every half hour or hour to do it until I actually get it done" (ReAnn — chronic illness, 20+ alarms)
- "you can have the alarm continue for up to 2 hours… fantastic for heavy sleepers" (Aaron)

### 3. Reliability is the #1 kill reason — and the #1 praise
- Every 1–2 star review is a missed/silent alarm: "not reliable, you will be late for work" (Najee), "too many missed alarms" (Jim), "a video ad played and prevented the alarm from sounding… 3 people late for an important meeting" (Pig), battery-saver kills, silent when app is open (Ayame — dangerous while driving).
- Every long-term 5-star says the same word: "never failed me in 3 years", "goes off under all circumstances", "completely reliable".
- iOS relevance: Remi's AlarmKit alarms are daemon-scheduled — survive app termination, ring on silent. This is a *true* differentiator against this corpus; say it.

### 4. Ads at the alarm moment are hated violently
- "who the heck wants an advertisement right after an alarm goes off!?" (Jack, 102 helpful)
- "you can't be an alarm clock app and screw up your one job because you're prioritizing ads" (Pig)
- "sick of ads everywhere and definitely don't want to see them on my alarm clock" (Katherine)
- Remi has zero ads → belongs in the listing copy explicitly.

### 5. The audience self-identifies: forgetful, ADHD, chronic illness, heavy sleepers
- "For an ADHDer with no internal clock, this is pretty great… tells me the time and what I need to do" (G A, 53 helpful)
- "I'm a pretty bad ADD dude. I set alarms for EVERYTHING" (Jay)
- "terrible memory & need to set up alarms for every event in my life… about 30 alarms" (Will)
- "living with several chronic illnesses… at least 20 different alarms" (ReAnn)
- Validates the `adhd` hidden keyword and the "For people who forget a lot" caption.

### 6. Actual use cases named (mirror these in screenshots)
Meds (most common), water/hydration (hourly), bins/trash, bills (monthly), doctor appointments,
feed the dog, school bus, birthdays, wake-ups. The Today-screen capture (Drink Water / Take Out
Trash / Take Pills) is a 1:1 mirror of this list — keep exactly that content in shots.

### 7. Voice personality delights
- "a British man reminding me of things really makes my day. I named him Basil" (Debi)
- "I love hearing the voice… you can choose the voice too" (Pamela)
- Beatrice (EN+AR) is Remi's version of this — a personality, not a TTS feature.

### 8. Alarm screen should show the reminder TEXT big
- "give me a huge display of the alarm TEXT! An alarm is a reminder — this app has the reminder
  in small letters at the top" (Peter T.) → feeds OLD-70 (alarm banner design): the reminder
  title is the hero element, not the clock.

### 9. Schedule flexibility gets specific praise
- "daily, weekly, day of week, multiples of days weeks or months" (Kelly, 129 helpful)
- "skip before the event if I've completed the task" (Kelly) — skip-one-occurrence is a loved QoL feature (also Ajay: "you can skip alarms and clear skips later")
- "hourly reminders to hydrate, daily wake up, monthly bills" (K)

### 10. Misc product notes for later
- Snooze lock-out complaints (Scott) — app usable while snoozed matters; Remi already is.
- Watch support requested repeatedly (Andrea, G J) — future.
- Search across many alarms (Will: 30 alarms) — future, list view already helps.
- Accidental dismiss while half-asleep (Carla) — slide-to-stop on iOS 26.1 solves this natively.
