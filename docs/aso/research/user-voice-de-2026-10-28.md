# User voice, German-speaking markets (DE/AT/CH): do people want a Remi?

Researched 2026-10-28. Every URL below was fetched or seen in a search result during this session. Google Play quotes come from the local scrape `docs/aso/competitors/talking-alarm/reviews.csv` (pulled 2026-10-07), and each one cites its `reviewId`. The store page itself was fetched today.

## 1. Demand verdict

**Yes: the need is real and recurring, but people don't talk about it much.** The strongest evidence is behavioural, not forum chatter. "Sprechender Wecker Plus" (Android, 10 Mio.+ downloads, 127.000 ratings) has about 3,200 German-language reviews, and a steady minority use it exactly as Remi works: *Termine/Tabletten werden angesagt*. Those users are mostly people on medication or older people, plus people who are simply forgetful. Blind and low-vision buyers are served almost entirely by hardware "sprechende Wecker". People with ADHS say "Wecker für alles" but never ask for speech.

On iPhone the explicit demand is thin: a handful of Apple Community and forum posts asking for loud or spoken reminders. iOS 26.2's "Dringend" reminders now cover the *loud* half of the need, but not the *spoken* half.

## 5. Context and gaps

- **Competitors.** iOS 26.2 "Dringend" reminders ring like an alarm, even when muted (https://support.apple.com/de-de/102484), but they don't speak. "Sprechender Wecker: Uhrzeit" (id6756029103) only announces the time and has no ratings yet (https://apps.apple.com/de/app/id6756029103). id1071899483 lets users record their own voice as the reminder sound.
- **WakeBuddy.** No German iOS listing found. Search only surfaced the Android "WakeBuddy: Talking Alarm Clock" (com.ahcmad.wakebuddy) and wakebuddy.ai, a call-you service. I couldn't verify the 2026-10-26 launch.
- **Discarded:** Reddit links with `?tl=de` (machine translations of English threads) and English r/ADHD threads (wrong language). Apple Community threads 254853620 and 255454019 returned an error page. Several App Store review feeds (id6445911723, 436647496, 431500634) were empty.
- **Thin spots:** I found no German-language r/ADHS, r/de or gutefrage thread asking for spoken reminders. YouTube was not reached. Senior and caregiver demand shows up mostly in care-portal copy and hardware listings, not first-person posts. The Play data is Android, and Play doesn't report country, so DE, AT and CH can't be separated.
