Privacy Policy

VoiceReminder · Last Updated: December 29, 2025

Overview

VoiceReminder is a voice-based reminder application that allows you to speak your reminders and receive audio notifications when they're due. This privacy policy explains how we handle your data.

Data We Collect

Voice Recordings: When you create a reminder, your voice is temporarily recorded and sent to our servers for processing. The audio is used to:

  • Convert your speech to text (using OpenAI Whisper)
  • Extract reminder details (time, date, message)
  • Generate a text-to-speech audio file for the notification

After processing, voice recordings are not permanently stored on our servers.

Data Storage

Local Storage: Your reminders (text, schedules, notification sounds) are stored locally on your device only. We do not sync reminders to the cloud.

No Account Required: The app does not require you to create an account. No personal information (name, email, phone number) is collected.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services for processing:

  • OpenAI – For speech-to-text transcription and natural language processing
  • ElevenLabs – For text-to-speech audio generation
  • Convex – Backend processing infrastructure

These services process your voice data only for the purpose of creating your reminder. Please refer to their respective privacy policies for more details.

Permissions

The app requests the following permissions:

  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): Required to record your voice when creating reminders
  • Notifications: Required to alert you when reminders are due

What We Do NOT Do

  • Sell your data to third parties
  • Use your data for advertising
  • Track your location
  • Store your voice recordings permanently

Children's Privacy

VoiceReminder is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last Updated" date above.
