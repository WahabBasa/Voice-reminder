3. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
Voice-first reminders: speak a reminder, the app transcribes and schedules it,
and at the due time it rings and speaks the reminder aloud. For adults who rely
on reminders and miss silent notifications. Free, with an optional Remi Pro
subscription (monthly or annual) for premium scheduling. No ads.

4. SETUP
No account or sample files needed. Launch, accept the AI consent sheet, allow
microphone and alarm access, tap the mic and speak ("remind me to drink water
in five minutes"). The reminder rings and speaks at the set time; Later snoozes,
Done completes. Paywall: Settings > upgrade row. Restore Purchases: Settings.

5. EXTERNAL SERVICES
Convex (backend), OpenAI (speech-to-text and reminder parsing), Speechify and
ElevenLabs (text-to-speech), RevenueCat (subscriptions on StoreKit), Sentry
(crash reports). Voice data is used only to create and speak reminders. No
tracking, no third-party ads.
