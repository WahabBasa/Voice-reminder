## Fixed reminder lifecycle edge cases + scheduling debug - (12:04)

**Status**: ✅ Behavior corrected, builds passing

Tracked down two reminder regressions and patched both in app logic:

- One-time reminders were disappearing from both **All** and **Completed** if they fired and were ignored.  
  Now they stay visible in **All** until explicitly completed, and overdue one-time reminders are auto-recorded as `missed` in history during startup sync.
- The home Completed feed was filtering only `completed`; it now includes both `completed` and `missed`.
- Repeating reminders created on the same day after their configured time (example: Sunday 7:00 AM created Sunday 9:00 AM) were showing as immediately delayed.  
  `getDueTimestamp()` for daily/weekly/custom now always resolves to the next future occurrence.
- Added a scheduling debug log in `scheduleReminder()` to print source, schedule type, now/trigger timestamps, and delta minutes.
