## "5 active reminders" with an empty list — diagnosed, ruled, fixed, shipped - (00:30-11:45)

**Status**: ✅ OLD-117..120 live via OTA 5f47c193 + dev Convex push; awaiting device verification

User hit the free cap after "setting and clearing five alarms". Turned out "active" was
computed three different ways: the mic-tap gate (`lib/reminderActive.ts:isReminderActive`,
via `lib/usage.ts`) said a one-off is active until the ledger has a `completed` entry; the
store gate (private `isReminderActiveForGate` in `lib/store.ts`) said missed OR past-due =
inactive; the launch cleanup removed only completed ones. A one-off that rang out on an
earlier day sat in the list forever (Today only shows one-offs dated today —
`lib/dayOccurrences.ts:93`), invisible, counted. A probe test at the real seam went red on

15:52/15:54/16:14, Cancel Subscriptions 08-25 18:00 — plus one dated 08-31 = six active).

Grilled the user on the product rule instead of just picking one. Decision: **a one-off is
owed until ticked or deleted; ringing out changes nothing** — `missed` in the ledger is a
record, not an ending. So the count was honest; the bug was visibility. That flipped the
fix from "missed = finished" to "show them".
