## Phase 11 Architecture Restructure - (19:00 - 19:17)

**Status**: ✅ Done

Rewrote Phase 11 (Monetization) with a simplified local-first architecture:

1. **Local-first decision** - Reminders stay on device, not synced to Convex. Convex only used for AI processing (Whisper → GPT → TTS). Enables full offline functionality.

3. **New dependency chain:**

4. **Updated for consistency:**
