# Devlog - 2026-08-06

## Found why reminders never fire until the app opens - (08:30-15:30)

**Status**: ✅ Full chain verified on S21 — dark phone woke itself, full-screen alarm + voice at 15:16:00.3

Four months of "reminders only play when I open the app" came down to Samsung deep-sleep force-stopping the app: force-stop cancels every AlarmManager registration AND blocks BOOT_COMPLETED, so notifee's triggers only came back when the app was manually opened (caught it live in logcat — tapped the icon at 08:31:57, two April alarms fired at 08:32:03). Battery exemption was the missing lever.

Chased three red herrings worth remembering:
- USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT "rejections" in appops were just Android suppressing full-screen while the screen is on/unlocked (`NO_FSI_NO_HUN_OR_KEYGUARD` in SystemUI logs). Every "failed" test had the screen on. Screen-off test worked first try.
- Notifee's "no notification data found" at fire time was OUR bug: `refreshNotificationWithAudio` did cancel-then-recreate; swipe-away in the gap loses the alarm. Create-with-same-id replaces atomically — never cancel first.
- Volumes/permissions were all fine.

Fixes:
- `components/PermissionPrompt.tsx` — third row "Run in Background": direct REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS dialog (expo-intent-launcher + manifest permission), AppState-based refresh, flex text sizing
- `lib/notificationDecisions.ts` — `isStaleDelivery` (>30 min late = record missed, don't ring; the dead `shouldRecordAsMissedInstead` guard finally has a working sibling)
- `lib/notifications.ts` — staleness guard wired into DELIVERED; atomic refresh
- Test suite passed 251/251 after; also fixed a date-dependent schedule test (fake timers, not Date.now spy)
