  network (`46:f0:71:74:59:63`), never the hardware MAC in the record. Saved the USB
  record into `~/.pymobiledevice3/<udid>.plist` and patched its MAC (backup `*.hwmac.bak`).

  background shell can't answer. Wrote the wrapper instead: connects by IP with the record
  and reuses the CLI's own `syslog_live` formatting/filters. Any pymobiledevice3 bonjour
  call on Windows needs `WindowsSelectorEventLoopPolicy` or discovery returns nothing.

Verified: paired session over Wi-Fi, ~4k lines in 11 s. `--ip` skips discovery.
